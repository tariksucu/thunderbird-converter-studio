Converter Studio

Converts the attachments you choose with your Converter Studio account. Most photos and documents cost 1 credit; free accounts get 25 credits a day.

“Fit attachments for sending” needs no key for photos: they are made smaller on this computer. With a key, a PDF, video or sound that still does not fit is made smaller by Converter Studio, and you see the price first.

Create a key on your account page. The key is kept with Thunderbird's storage.local: in this Thunderbird profile on this computer only, not synced, not encrypted (like the add-on's other settings), and removed with the add-on. It is only sent to the Converter Studio address below.
